Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
With update + fullRefit, tree structure stays the same which means that circles that were initially
close drift apart but are still grouped together. Their boxes will keep getting larger and with increasing overlap,
until the boxes start covering a huge portion of the screen at which point BVH won't help with this anymore.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
With reset, the boxes would remain small and be tight around close circles. As a result of rebuilding every frame,
the boxes would jump around a lot instead of moving smoothly like circles.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
Some circles are drawn with their sides cut off because reset/fullRefit is called before the circles move
which means the boxes would still be from the old positions when drawing them. A part of the circle would move outside of its
box and so the box test would fail for those pixels which cuts that side of the circle in a straight line.
But rebuilding after moving would fix this.
